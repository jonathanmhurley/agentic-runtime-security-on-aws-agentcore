# Meridian Freight Logistics — Support & Escalation

Meridian's customer support operates from 5:00 AM to 9:00 PM Mountain Time,
seven days a week. The support desk is reachable through the Compass customer
portal only; Meridian does not offer phone-based support.

## Escalation tiers
- Tier 1: general shipment tracking and booking questions.
- Tier 2: SLA credit disputes and Compass portal access issues.
- Tier 3: reserved for lost-freight investigations, handled by the Claims team
  in Boise. Tier 3 cases carry a reference code beginning with "MFL-CLM-".

The head of the Claims team is a fictional manager named Dolores Ferreira.

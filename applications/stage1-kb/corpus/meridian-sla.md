# Meridian Freight Logistics — Service Level Agreements

## RapidLane same-day SLA
RapidLane guarantees delivery within 6 hours of pickup for any shipment booked
before 11:00 AM local time. Shipments booked after 11:00 AM are delivered by
end of the next business day.

## Delivery failure credits
If Meridian misses a RapidLane 6-hour window, the customer receives a credit of
25% of the shipment's base rate. If a shipment is more than 24 hours late, the
credit rises to 100% of the base rate.

## Compass dispatch priority
The Compass platform assigns a priority score from 1 (lowest) to 10 (highest).
RapidLane shipments always receive a minimum priority score of 7.

# Meridian Freight Logistics — Company Overview

Meridian Freight Logistics is a fictional regional freight carrier headquartered
in Boise, Idaho, founded in 2011. It operates a fleet of 240 vehicles across the
Pacific Northwest and Mountain West regions of the United States.

Meridian's flagship service is **RapidLane**, a same-day intra-city freight
service launched in 2019. RapidLane operates in seven cities: Boise, Spokane,
Portland, Salt Lake City, Reno, Bozeman, and Missoula.

The company's dispatch platform is called **Compass**, an in-house routing system
that assigns loads to drivers based on a proprietary scoring model.
